V4 answers:
At 10,000 rows, the pandas transform alone gives only a small speed improvement (about 1.1x), so the benefit is modest at this size. However, the bulk load using execute_values is clearly worthwhile, giving about a 3.6x speed-up.

In this run, loading takes the most time. Extraction took about 0.111s, transformation about 0.05s, while the bulk fact load took about 0.45s.
